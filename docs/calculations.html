Transmission Line — Theory & Calculations

1. Conductor Geometry

Each phase consists of a bundle of \(n_s\) (\(\texttt{scCount}\)) sub-conductors, each stranded ACSR conductor with \(n\) (\(\texttt{scStrands}\)) strands of diameter \(d\) (\(\texttt{strandDiaM}\)).

Overall conductor radius (outer radius of stranded conductor):

\[ r = \frac{(2n_{\text{layers}} - 1) \cdot d}{2}, \quad n_{\text{layers}} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{(4n-1)/3}}{2} \]

Geometric mean radius (GMR / DSL) of a stranded conductor:

\[ \text{GMR} = r \cdot e^{-1/4} \approx 0.7788\,r \]

Bundle GMR / GMD for \(n_s\) sub-conductors spaced \(d_s\) (\(\texttt{scSpacingM}\)) apart:

\[ D_{SL} = \sqrt[n_s]{\text{GMR} \cdot \prod_{k=1}^{n_s-1} d_{sk}} \]

For a 2-bundle: \(D_{SL} = \sqrt{\text{GMR}\cdot d_s}\)  |  3-bundle: \(D_{SL} = \sqrt[3]{\text{GMR}\cdot d_s^2}\)  |  4-bundle: \(D_{SL} = \sqrt[4]{\text{GMR}\cdot d_s^3 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,d_s}\)

2. Equivalent Phase Spacing

For a symmetrical (\(\texttt{symmetric}=1\)) system all inter-phase distances equal \(D\) (\(\texttt{phaseSpacingM}\)):

\[ D_{eq} = D \]

For an unsymmetrical / transposed (\(\texttt{symmetric}=0\)) system with distances \(D_{ab}\), \(D_{bc}\), \(D_{ca}\):

\[ D_{eq} = \sqrt[3]{D_{ab}\,D_{bc}\,D_{ca}} \]

3. Inductance & Capacitance per Phase per km

\[ L = \frac{2\times 10^{-4}}{n_s}\ln\!\left(\frac{D_{eq}}{D_{SL}}\right) \quad \text{H/km/phase} \quad (\texttt{Lphkm}) \] \[ C = \frac{2\pi\varepsilon_0}{\ln\!\left(\dfrac{D_{eq}}{r_{\,\text{bundle}}}\right)} \times 10^{3} \quad \text{F/km/phase} \quad (\texttt{Cphkm}) \]

where \(r_{\text{bundle}}\) is the bundle equivalent radius (same formula as \(D_{SL}\) but using the outer radius \(r\) of a sub-conductor instead of GMR).

4. Reactances of the Line

\[ X_L = \omega L \ell = 2\pi f \cdot L \cdot \ell \quad \Omega \quad (\texttt{Xl}) \] \[ X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C \ell} \quad \Omega \quad (\texttt{Xc}) \]

where \(\ell\) (\(\texttt{lineLengthKm}\)) is the line length in km.

5. Series Resistance

Total series resistance per phase for the full line length:

\[ R = \frac{r_{\text{sc}}}{n_s} \cdot \ell \quad \Omega \]

where \(r_{\text{sc}}\) (\(\texttt{resSCPerKm}\)) is the resistance of one sub-conductor per km.

6. ABCD Parameters & Line Models

The line is represented by its two-port ABCD (transmission) matrix:

\[ \begin{pmatrix} V_S \\ I_S \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} V_R \\ I_R \end{pmatrix} \]

6a. Short Line (\(\texttt{model}=0\))

Total series impedance: \(\mathbf{Z} = R + jX_L\)

\[ A = D = 1, \quad B = \mathbf{Z}, \quad C = 0 \]

6b. Nominal \(\pi\) Model (\(\texttt{model}=1\))

Total shunt admittance: \(\mathbf{Y} = j/X_C\)

\[ A = D = 1 + \frac{\mathbf{Z}\mathbf{Y}}{2}, \quad B = \mathbf{Z}, \quad C = \mathbf{Y}\!\left(1 + \frac{\mathbf{Z}\mathbf{Y}}{4}\right) \]

6c. Distributed (Exact) Model (\(\texttt{model}=2\))

Propagation constant and characteristic impedance per unit length:

\[ \gamma = \sqrt{(r + j\omega L_{\text{km}})(j\omega C_{\text{km}})}, \quad Z_c = \sqrt{\frac{r + j\omega L_{\text{km}}}{j\omega C_{\text{km}}}} \] \[ A = D = \cosh(\gamma\ell), \quad B = Z_c\sinh(\gamma\ell), \quad C = \frac{\sinh(\gamma\ell)}{Z_c} \]

7. Sending End Voltage & Current

Receiving end phase voltage (nominal, taken as reference):

\[ \mathbf{V}_R = \frac{V_{\text{nom}}}{\sqrt{3}}\angle 0° \quad \text{kV} \]

Receiving end current (lagging power factor \(\cos\phi = \texttt{recvPF}\)):

\[ \mathbf{I}_R = \frac{P_R}{3\,|\mathbf{V}_R|\cos\phi}\angle{-\phi} \]

Sending end quantities (\(\texttt{Vs}\), \(\texttt{Is}\)):

\[ \mathbf{V}_S = A\,\mathbf{V}_R + B\,\mathbf{I}_R, \quad \mathbf{I}_S = C\,\mathbf{V}_R + D\,\mathbf{I}_R \]

Line-to-line sending end voltage:

\[ V_{S,LL} = \sqrt{3}\,|\mathbf{V}_S| \quad \text{kV} \]

8. Percentage Voltage Regulation

\[ \text{VR} = \frac{|\mathbf{V}_S|/|A| - |\mathbf{V}_R|}{|\mathbf{V}_R|}\times 100\% \]

9. Power Loss & Transmission Efficiency

\[ P_{\text{loss}} = 3\,|I_R|^2\,R \quad \text{MW} \quad (\texttt{loss}) \] \[ \eta = \frac{P_R}{P_R + P_{\text{loss}}} \quad (\texttt{eta}) \]

10. Charging Current

\[ \mathbf{I}_c = \mathbf{I}_S - \mathbf{I}_R \quad \text{A} \quad (\texttt{Ic}) \]

11. Surge Impedance & SIL

Lossless approximation (\(R=0\), \(G=0\)):

\[ Z_c = \sqrt{\frac{L_{\text{km}}}{C_{\text{km}}}} \quad \Omega \quad (\texttt{Zc}) \] \[ \text{SIL} = \frac{V_{\text{nom}}^2}{Z_c} \quad \text{MW (3-phase)} \quad (\texttt{SIL}) \]
